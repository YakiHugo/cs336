# 2026-08-11

## RMSNorm ablation

- The owner predicted the correct failure direction: without RMSNorm, internal values can
  grow and training becomes more unstable. Refined “numerical instability” into activation,
  logit, and gradient scale drift, which can be severe before any `inf` or `NaN` appears.
- Added a default-preserving model configuration for `normalization=none`. It removes both
  block RMSNorms and the final RMSNorm; the normal pre-norm state dict/API remain unchanged.
  The official suite remains **46 passed, 2 platform skips**, and Ruff is green.
- Controlled 100-step curves used the same B32/T256 data, seed, evaluation batches, warmup,
  and cosine schedule as the prior LR sweep. At the old short-sweep optimum `3e-3`, no-norm
  validation loss was **2,322 / 6,559 / 197,508 / 63,151** at steps 25/50/75/100. Raw
  gradient norm reached 705 at step 20, 446k at step 30, and 19.7M at step 50 despite clipping.
- Lowering peak LR to `1e-3` restored a smooth curve ending at **3.556**; lowering again to
  `3e-4` ended at **4.279**, stable but too slow. In this short budget, `1e-3` is the best
  tested no-norm LR.
- A fixed-input initialization diagnostic measured block-output RMS of
  `1.08→1.18→1.28→1.40` with pre-norm versus `1.08→1.26→1.93→14.53` with no norm; logit
  RMS was 0.31 versus 4.48. This directly connects the architecture to the observed
  optimization instability.
- Added a dependency-free SVG curve renderer and captured the scored deliverable at
  `notes/a1/figures/layer_norm_ablation.svg`. Learning remains in progress pending one
  transfer check; post-norm is next.
